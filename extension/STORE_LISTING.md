## Short Description (132 characters max)
Never lose your AI flow state. Save project context and switch between Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini in one click.

## Long Description

Continuum AI is your persistent memory layer across all AI tools. Never lose your conversation context when switching between different AI assistants or hitting usage limits.

### Key Features

**🔄 Seamless AI Switching**
- Automatically detect when you hit usage limits on Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini
- One-click handoff to continue your conversation on another platform
- Context-aware transitions that preserve your project state

**💾 Project Memory**
- Save checkpoints of your conversations with a single click
- Track decisions, rejected ideas, open tasks, and constraints
- Build a knowledge base for your projects over time

**🎯 Smart Context Extraction**
- AI-powered extraction of key information from conversations
- Automatic identification of decisions and trade-offs
- Delta tracking to see what changed between sessions

**⚡ Auto-Injection**
- Seamlessly inject your project context into new AI conversations
- Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, and Perplexity
- No manual copy-pasting required

### How It Works

1. **Install the Extension** - Add Continuum to your Chrome browser
2. **Create a Project** - Sign in and create your first project on the Continuum dashboard
3. **Save Checkpoints** - Click the Continuum indicator to save your conversation state
4. **Switch AI** - When you hit a limit, click "Switch AI" to continue on another platform
5. **Stay in Flow** - Your context is automatically preserved and injected

## Permissions

### activeTab
**Why needed:** To read conversation content from the active AI platform tab when you save a checkpoint or switch platforms.

### storage
**Why needed:** To store your authentication token, active project selection, and settings locally in your browser.

### tabs
**Why needed:** To open new tabs when switching to a different AI platform and to manage tab state during handoff.

### scripting
**Why needed:** To inject the Continuum indicator UI and handoff context into AI platform pages.

### Host Permissions
**Why needed:** To run content scripts on supported AI platforms (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity) and communicate with the Continuum API.

## Privacy Policy

Continuum AI respects your privacy. We collect only the data necessary to provide our services:

- **Conversation Content:** Only when you explicitly save a checkpoint
- **Project Metadata:** Project names, descriptions, and type
- **Authentication:** Secure tokens for account access

We never sell your data and use industry-standard encryption to protect your information. You can export or delete your data at any time through the Continuum dashboard.
